## 🟢 The Big Idea (ELI5)

You're going **backwards from FOIL.** Someone multiplied two binomials and got `x² + 7x + 12`. Your job: figure out what the two binomials were.

The answer is always: `(x + ___)(x + ___)`. You just need to find the two numbers that go in the blanks.

**Magic question:** "What two numbers **multiply to c** AND **add to b**?"

For `x² + 7x + 12`: what two numbers multiply to 12 and add to 7? **3 and 4.**
So `x² + 7x + 12 = (x + 3)(x + 4)`. Done.

## 🧾 The Recipe

For `x² + bx + c` (where the leading coefficient is **1**):

1. **Check GCF first.** (Always. See Topic 3.)
2. **Identify b and c** in `x² + bx + c`.
3. **Find two numbers that multiply to c AND add to b.**
4. **Write them in the blanks:** `(x + ___)(x + ___)`.
5. **Check by FOIL** to make sure it works.

### Sign Rules — Memorize These

| Sign of c | Sign of b | What the two numbers look like |
|---|---|---|
| **+** (positive) | **+** (positive) | Both **positive**: x² + 7x + 12 → +3 and +4 |
| **+** (positive) | **−** (negative) | Both **negative**: x² − 7x + 12 → −3 and −4 |
| **−** (negative) | **+** (positive) | One **+**, one **−**. Bigger one is **+**: x² + 5x − 6 → +6 and −1 |
| **−** (negative) | **−** (negative) | One **+**, one **−**. Bigger one is **−**: x² − 5x − 6 → −6 and +1 |

---

## 🧠 Mnemonic — "MA": Multiply, Add

**M** before **A** in the alphabet — and **M**ultiply before **A**dd in the search.

Or: **"Multiply to c, Add to b"** — say it out loud every time.

For signs: **"Same signs = both same as b. Different signs = bigger one wears b's sign."**

## ✏️ Worked Example

**Factor:** `x² − 9x + 20`

Step 1: GCF? No (no number divides 1, −9, and 20).
Step 2: b = −9, c = +20.
Step 3: Need two numbers that:
- **Multiply to +20**
- **Add to −9**

Try pairs of 20: (1, 20), (2, 10), (4, 5). The one that adds to 9 is **4 and 5**.
Both must be **negative** (so the product stays positive, but the sum is negative): **−4 and −5**.

Step 4: `(x − 4)(x − 5)` ✅

Step 5: Check by FOIL: `x·x − 5x − 4x + 20 = x² − 9x + 20` ✓

## 💎 Diamond Method (visual scaffold)

```
        12   ← product (c)
       /  \
      3    4    ← the two numbers
       \  /
        7   ← sum (b)
```

Put **c** at the top and **b** at the bottom. Fill in the two side numbers that work. Use this if listing pairs feels random.

---

## ⚠️ Common Traps

1. **Wrong signs.** Re-read the sign rules table. Test with FOIL every single time.
2. **Forgetting to check GCF first.** `2x² + 14x + 24` — factor out 2 first: `2(x² + 7x + 12) = 2(x + 3)(x + 4)`.
3. **Trying random pairs in random order.** Use a system: list pairs of c smallest to largest, check which adds to b.
4. **`x² + bx − c` confusion** (when c is negative). The two numbers have **different signs**. The one with the bigger absolute value matches the sign of b.

4. `x² − 3x − 10`
5. `x² + 11x + 24`

## ✅ Answers

1. `(x + 3)(x + 5)` — both positive: multiply to 15, add to 8 → 3, 5
2. `(x − 2)(x − 3)` — both negative: multiply to 6, add to −5 → −2, −3
3. `(x + 4)(x − 2)` — different signs, bigger one positive: multiply to −8, add to +2 → +4, −2
4. `(x − 5)(x + 2)` — different signs, bigger one negative: multiply to −10, add to −3 → −5, +2
5. `(x + 3)(x + 8)` — both positive: multiply to 24, add to 11 → 3, 8

(If you got 4 or 5 right → ✓ done. **If you missed the sign ones (3, 4), redo those tomorrow.**)
