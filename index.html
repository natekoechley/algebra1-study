Things to try

  • Standard form: Set b=0 and slide c. See the parabola move up and down.
  • Vertex form: Watch the vertex move when you change h and k. (Notice: h is the OPPOSITE sign of the move direction.)
  • Intercept form: Slide p and q — see how the roots move with them.
  • All forms: Make a negative — see the parabola flip upside down.

🚨 "I'm Stuck" Cheat Sheet

🚨 PANIC CARD 🚨

It's okay. Frozen brain happens. Do this in order:

  1. Breathe. Out longer than in. 3 times.
  2. Name the topic. "This is a ___ problem." Open that topic.
  3. Copy the example. Don't try to remember — just follow the recipe.
  4. Still stuck? Skip it. Move on. Come back.
  5. Text Nate. A photo. He'll send a hint, not the answer.

Esme's Rule: Stuck for >3 min = move on. Time is the enemy of confidence.
